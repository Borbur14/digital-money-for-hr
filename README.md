# Digital money for HR: open glossary and job map (2026)

Two free datasets for HR, reward and finance teams that need to understand stablecoins, tokens and digital pay. Both are in English and Spanish, and every row links to the guide on [borbur.net](https://borbur.net/) that explains it with sources.

By **[Borja Burguillos](https://borbur.net/about/)**, author of *[Stablecoins for a Better World](https://borbur.net/books/stablecoins-for-a-better-world/)* and *[Why Bitcoin Had to Exist](https://borbur.net/books/why-bitcoin-had-to-exist/)*. Licence: [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE).

### The glossary

Five groups: money (15 terms), rules and regulators (14), issuing (11), technology (10) and pay (10). It covers, among others: stablecoin, payment stablecoin, e-money token (EMT), payment token, dirham stablecoin, tokenised deposit, CBDC, de-peg, redemption run, reserves, attestation, custody, MiCA, the GENIUS Act, the UAE Central Bank's Payment Token Services Regulation, VARA, the Wage Protection System, the EU Pay Transparency Directive, Spain's Real Decreto-ley 39/2026, the EU AI Act, the Travel Rule, token grants, vesting, lock-ups, stablecoin payroll and FX risk.

Columns: `id, group, term_en, definition_en, why_it_matters_for_hr_en, term_es, definition_es, why_it_matters_for_hr_es, guide_en, guide_es`.

Legal terms follow the texts in force as of 22 November 2026. Read the linked guide for the source of each rule.

### The job map

Five job families: compliance, risk and regulatory affairs; custody and key security; issuance, reserves and treasury; tokenisation and product; corporate digital treasury. Each has four levels (specialist, senior or lead, head of, executive).

Columns: `family, level, level_name, example_titles, family_es, example_titles_es, typical_background`.
